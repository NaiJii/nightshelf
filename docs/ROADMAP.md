# Feature roadmap

## Implemented — awaiting phone review

- **6 — Reader page scrubber:** a slider shown with the reader controls, with a saved-panel preview while dragging and position within long webtoon panels. Novels show a paragraph preview. Releasing commits the jump; cancelling preserves the current position. Previewing does not save progress or mark a chapter read. The controls retain their size when zoomed and sit above the bottom reader bar.

- **Home reading shortcut:** resume the most recently read story at its saved position, or offer to download its next known unread chapter.
- **Download drawer:** open queue progress, ordering, pause/resume and stop controls from the compact percentage indicator; keep browsing headers compact.
- **Chapter-number jump:** tap the Chapters heading to find an exact chapter, including decimal numbers, and scroll to it.
- **Inline panel repair:** retain failed panels in place, retry only the chosen original image from the public chapter page, and preserve the visible reading anchor.
- **Search context:** show source, language and content type on cards; keep recent searches per provider and allow one-tap clearing.
- **Availability feedback:** show source-reported restricted chapters with a lock, exclude them from downloads, and explain when a missing chapter needs internet.

Returning from the reader already preserves the mounted series screen's chapter-list position, filters and selection; this behavior is retained.

## Dropped

- **9 — Cross-provider linking:** removed from the planned scope at the user's request.
